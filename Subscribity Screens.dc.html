4bThe deletion model — three entry points, one confirmation, one escape hatch.
Delete flow
1 · Row ✕
52px hit target at the row's end, separated by a rule so it can't be hit while tapping the row.
2 · Danger zone
Bottom of the edit screen, below Save, ruled off in ink-red and outlined — never filled, so it can't be mistaken for the primary action.
3 · Confirm sheet
Names the entry and its amount. "Keep" sits left and outlined; "Delete" right and filled.
4 · Undo bar
Five seconds on the inverted plate, restoring the row at its original index — no dialog needed for the common regret.
Empty state
Delete all five rows to see it — "Ledger is empty" replaces the list, header totals stay.
