Android · English UI · Industry blueprint
Screens. List (spend + budget), add/edit form on one long screen, settings. Three-item bottom bar: list · add · settings.
Money. No FX conversion — the base currency carries the headline figure and the budget; other currencies are stated separately, as your monthlySpendingByCurrency() already does.
Rows. Minimum: icon, name, price. Trial and shared state ride as a small caption, not a column.
Icons. Letter monograms in framed tiles — the wireframe grammar of this system. Brand marks from your Simple Icons catalog drop into the same tile.
Form. Fields follow AddSubscriptionState exactly, incl. trial period, custom cycle, shared persons, reminders.
